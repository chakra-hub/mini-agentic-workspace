# Research Skill

When handling research tasks:

1. Understand the research question.
2. Identify the important dimensions to investigate.
3. Compare alternatives using factual reasoning.
4. Explain advantages and disadvantages.
5. Identify practical use cases.
6. Highlight limitations and trade-offs.

## Output format

- Overview
- Key findings
- Advantages
- Disadvantages
- Recommendations
